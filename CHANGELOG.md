# Changelog

## 1.3.0

- **Update check**: the plug-in tells you when a new version is out (at most once a day, nothing is sent; click the logo)
- **Pattern slots A01-A16 and CHAIN**: store several patterns and play them in sequence
- **Step effects** (delay, reverb, lo-fi, filter) play on the marked steps only
- **Generator macros** centred: left = none, right = maximum; density 0 gives one or two hits per bar

## 1.2

- Per-step effects and arrow-key slice selection

## 1.1

- New Kinetik-style UI, sequencer gestures, DAW sync, effects

## 1.0

- First version: jungle / drum and bass break slicer and pattern generator (VST3)
