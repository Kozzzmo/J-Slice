# Changelog

## 1.4.1

- Larger, bolder text: every label in IBM Plex Mono SemiBold, 8 % larger; small values 15 % larger.

## 1.4.0

- **J-Slice is now KJNG-2** (Kinetik Jungle): new name in the window and in the music software. Projects made with
  J-Slice open unchanged (same plug-in identity); the plug-in files keep their name so an update replaces them.
- **RESET** button (next to SKIN): every setting, the 16 pattern slots and the chain go back to default; the loaded
  break and its slices stay. UNDO brings the edited pattern back.

## 1.3.3

- New Kinetik logo: the "Orbit" mark, in every skin, with a larger product name

## 1.3.2

- Safer project loading: slices outside the audio, an invalid tempo or bar count, an oversized embedded break or a
  "nan" gate in a damaged project are corrected instead of reading outside the audio (possible crash)

## 1.3.1

- NEON skin: the pink highlights are red

## 1.3.0

- **Update check**: the plug-in tells you when a new version is out (at most once a day, nothing is sent; click the logo)
- **Pattern slots A01-A16 and CHAIN**: store several patterns and play them in sequence
- **Step effects** (delay, reverb, lo-fi, filter) play on the marked steps only
- **Generator macros** centred: left = none, right = maximum; density 0 gives one or two hits per bar

## 1.2

- Per-step effects and arrow-key slice selection

## 1.1

- New Kinetik-style UI, sequencer gestures, DAW sync, effects

## 1.0

- First version: jungle / drum and bass break slicer and pattern generator (VST3)
